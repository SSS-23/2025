GYDA and Beijing International Institute for Digital Economy Governance Sign Strategic Partnership MOU

Location

Beijing

Date

April 16, 2026

Global Youth Development Alliance Partners with Leading Chinese Digital Economy Think Tank to Empower Youth in the Digital Era

The Global Youth Development Alliance (GYDA) and the Beijing International Institute for Digital Economy Governance (BIDEGA) today officially signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU), establishing a long-term strategic partnership in the digital economy sector.

A Powerful Alliance for Youth Empowerment

GYDA, an official advocate for the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), has been dedicated to connecting global youth with international organizations, governments, enterprises, and research institutions to promote technology equity and inclusive development. BIDEGA, established in 2022 under the guidance of the Beijing Municipal Government, is a high-end think tank platform initiated by relevant national ministries, top experts in the digital economy, leading enterprises, and renowned scholars.

This partnership will integrate GYDA's global youth network and international organizational resources with BIDEGA's professional capabilities and policy influence in digital economy governance, jointly building a bridge for global youth to participate in digital economy governance and technological innovation.

Deepening Cooperation for Shared Development

According to the MOU, both parties will conduct comprehensive cooperation in policy research, international exchange, talent development, and joint research, jointly promoting youth empowerment and sustainable development in the digital economy sector.

Both parties have established a normalized working coordination mechanism to jointly promote the implementation of specific business operations.

About Global Youth Development Alliance

The Global Youth Development Alliance (GYDA) is an international, non-profit organization legally registered in Paris, France, and an international youth development institution oriented towards the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). It is committed to connecting global youth with international organizations, governments, enterprises, and research institutions, building platforms for youth development, leadership cultivation, industry-academia-research transformation, and international exchange. GYDA promotes technology equity and inclusive development, assists youth in participating in global sustainable development actions, and contributes youth strength to the implementation of the UN 2030 Sustainable Development Agenda and the building of a community with a shared future for mankind.

About Beijing International Institute for Digital Economy Governance

With the mission of "empowering high-quality development of the digital economy and shaping the international governance discourse system," BIDEGA is committed to building a professional, full-time, and international high-end think tank platform. The Institute has established a professional service system covering data governance and cross-border compliance, policy formulation and standard setting, comprehensive enterprise globalization services, digital talent cultivation, and high-end international exchange platforms.
