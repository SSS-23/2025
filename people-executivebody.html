Brand and Relationship Division

Focused on the organization’s external communications, branding, partnerships, and public image.

Subordinate Departments:

  • Department of Communications – Responsible for brand communication, media relations, public relations activities, social media operations, and the creation and distribution of multilingual content.
  • Department of Partnerships and Engagement – Responsible for establishing and maintaining partnerships with governments, corporations, foundations, universities, and other external organizations, in order to expand resources and influence.

Program and Knowledge Division

Responsible for all externally oriented project implementation and knowledge output, ensuring effective execution and professional impact.

Subordinate Departments:

  • Department of Program Implementation – Responsible for translating project plans into actionable execution, coordinating with regional offices and local partners to ensure efficient project operations.
  • Department of Research and Publications – Responsible for conducting research on relevant topics, preparing reports and publications, and providing knowledge support and content output.

Institutional Integrity Division

Focused on the critical safeguards that sustain the organization’s operations, including core back-office functions such as finance, human resources, and legal affairs.

Subordinate Departments:

  • Department of Finance – Responsible for financial management, budget control, audit readiness, cross-border fund flows, and financial compliance.
  • Department of Legal and Compliance – Responsible for legal affairs, contract review, organizational compliance, international registrations, and risk control.
  • Department of Human Resources – Responsible for talent acquisition, staff development, performance evaluation, organizational culture building, DEI (Diversity, Equity & Inclusion) policies, and employee benefits.
